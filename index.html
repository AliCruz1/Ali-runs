2K27 guide

NBA 2K27 launched September 4, 2026 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC through Steam, and Nintendo Switch 2. Here is what to know before we run.

Before you book

  • You need NBA 2K27 and an NBA 2K account.
  • The City and every mode we play needs an internet connection. Only Play Now, MyNBA, and The W work offline.
  • On Xbox, online multiplayer needs Game Pass Essential, Premium, or Ultimate. Check what your console requires for online play.
  • Make a MyPLAYER first so you can walk into The City.
  • I play on Xbox (vStoppedALI). Any platform can book. I will confirm by email whether we can play together.

What is new this year

  • The City is co-ed for the first time. All modes are open to every MyPLAYER.
  • The REC rotates between five venues, including a High School Gym and an Outdoor Court.
  • Street Kings adds Rucker Park.
  • Crew HQ and Casual Corner are new spaces, and Daily Rivals tracks daily standings.
  • MyPLAYER Builder adds Builder Pass, Badge Tokens, and Synergy Boosts.
  • The NBA 2K HQ companion app lets you manage your crew, chat, and check leaderboards.
Game plan by mode

2s

Two players means no hiding. Set the pick and roll, make the defense pick a side, and talk on every switch. One of you has to be a real shooting threat.

3s

Fill three roles: a creator, a shooter, and a rebounder or rim protector. Space the floor, rotate on help defense, and do not all chase the ball at once.

Rec

Play your role, box out, and pass up bad shots. Use the mic to call matchups. Five players with one plan beat five players with five. I line up the squad, so a Rec booking may take a bit longer to confirm.

Everyone

Learn your shot timing, watch your stamina, contest without fouling, and keep your head after a bad quarter.

House rules

  • Be ready 10 minutes early. I wait 10 minutes past start time, then release the slot.
  • No quitting mid-game.
  • Good sportsmanship, no trash talk that crosses the line.
  • A mic is strongly encouraged but not required.
  • Cancel by replying to your confirmation email, ideally 2 hours ahead.
  • This is a casual run, not a coaching session, but I am happy to share tips.

FAQ

How do I know I am booked?

Your request goes straight to my email. I reply to the address you give to confirm the day, time, and how to add me as a friend. Check spam if you do not see it.

Can two people book the same time?

Possibly. This site cannot see other requests, so I confirm slots in the order they arrive and suggest another time if one is taken.

What time zone are the times in?

Eastern Time (New York). If your device is in another zone, your local time is shown next to each slot.

I am not on Xbox. Can we still run?

Book anyway. I will email you to check whether crossplay works for us. If it does not, no problem.

Does it cost anything?

No, the runs are free.

Do I need to be good?

No. Say your skill level in the notes and I will match the pace.
